1. Purpose
----------
These guidelines help employees choose the right communication channel and communicate clearly and professionally.

2. Choosing the Right Channel
-----------------------------
- Email - formal communication, external communication, approvals and anything that needs a record.
- NovaChat - quick questions and day-to-day team discussion.
- Video meetings - discussions that need back-and-forth conversation.
- Ticketing portals - requests to IT, HR or Finance (so they can be tracked).

3. Response Times
-----------------
- Reply to internal emails within 1 business day.
- Reply to customer emails within 4 business hours.
- NovaChat messages during core hours should be answered within 2 hours.

4. Meetings
-----------
- Every meeting must have an agenda shared in the invitation.
- Keep meetings to 30 minutes where possible.
- Share notes and action items within 24 hours.
- Avoid scheduling meetings outside core hours across time zones without agreement.

5. External Communication
-------------------------
Only the Corporate Communications team may speak to the media or post official statements on behalf of TechNova. Employees must not share confidential information on social media.

6. Tone and Etiquette
---------------------
Be respectful, clear and concise. Avoid writing in all capital letters. Do not use NovaChat or email to give critical feedback - discuss it privately in person or on a call.
