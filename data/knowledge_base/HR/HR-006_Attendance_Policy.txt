1. Purpose
----------
This policy explains working hours, how attendance is recorded, and what happens in case of late arrival or missing attendance.

2. Working Hours
----------------
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Core hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM; all employees must be available during core hours, whether working from the office or from home.

3. Recording Attendance
-----------------------
- In the office, attendance is recorded automatically when you swipe your ID card at the entrance.
- When working from home, mark your attendance in PeopleHub under 'My Attendance' before 10:00 AM.
- When on client visits or business travel, mark 'On Duty' in PeopleHub.

4. Late Arrival
---------------
Arriving after 10:00 AM is recorded as a late mark. 3 late marks in one calendar month result in a half-day of annual leave being deducted. Managers may excuse late marks caused by genuine emergencies.

5. Attendance Regularization
----------------------------
If you forgot to swipe or mark attendance, submit a regularization request in PeopleHub within 5 working days. After this period, the day is treated as leave without pay unless HR approves an exception.

6. Unplanned Absence
--------------------
If you cannot come to work unexpectedly, inform your manager by 10:00 AM by phone or NovaChat. Being absent for 3 consecutive working days without informing anyone is treated as unauthorized absence and may lead to disciplinary action.
