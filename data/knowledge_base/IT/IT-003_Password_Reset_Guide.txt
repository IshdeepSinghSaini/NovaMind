1. Password Rules
-----------------
- At least 12 characters long.
- Must contain an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character.
- Passwords expire every 90 days; you get reminders 14 days before.
- You cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords.
- After 5 wrong attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes.

2. Resetting Your Password (Self-Service)
-----------------------------------------
- Go to id.technova.internal from any browser.
- Click 'Forgot password'.
- Enter your TechNova email address.
- Approve the verification request in the NovaAuth app.
- Enter and confirm your new password.
- Sign in again on your laptop, NovaChat and email using the new password.

3. Changing Your Password Before It Expires
-------------------------------------------
Sign in to id.technova.internal, open 'Security' and click 'Change password'. On Windows you can also press Ctrl+Alt+Delete and choose 'Change a password' while connected to the office network or the VPN.

4. Lost Phone or MFA Problems
-----------------------------
If you lost the phone with NovaAuth, you cannot use self-service reset. Call the IT helpdesk at extension 4357. For security, the helpdesk will verify your identity using your employee ID and a confirmation from your reporting manager before resetting your MFA.

5. Safety Tips
--------------
- IT will never ask for your password by email, phone or chat.
- Use a passphrase such as three or four random words with numbers and symbols.
- Never write your password on paper or share it with colleagues.
